A Third-Person FPS & Visual Novel Hybrid

🌍 The Premise
In a world reclaimed by steel and circuitry, humanity has been sidelined by a global robotic uprising. You play as a survivor whose family has been kidnapped by the machine collective. To see them again, you must dismantle the mechanical hierarchy, starting with the power grid that fuels their dominance.

🎯 Objective
In this Vertical Slice, players explore the industrial ruins of the first city. Your mission is to locate and destroy the Main Power Source. However, the main core is shielded; you must first find and neutralize three smaller sub-cores distributed throughout the environment to leave the heart of the machine vulnerable.

🏙️ The Journey: Three Cities
The game is structured across three distinct urban environments, each representing a different stage of the machine takeover:

Ashveil (Current): The industrial gateway and the setting of this Vertical Slice.

Nullhaven: The cold, data-driven secondary hub where the rebellion thickens.

Verdex: The final overgrown citadel where the ultimate power source resides.

🎮 Gameplay Features
Hybrid Combat: Fast-paced third-person shooting mechanics.

Visual Novel Narrative: Deep story segments focusing on the protagonist's emotional journey and the search for his kidnapped family.

Tactical Progression: A "Destroy-to-Advance" loop centered on the three sub-cores.

Modular 3D Environment: Detailed interior sets including residential and industrial assets (Furniture, Walls, and Props).
